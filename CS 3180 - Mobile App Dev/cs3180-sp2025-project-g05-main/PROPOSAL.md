## Application

An app that estimates the remaining capacity of an ebike battery after a trip, based on weather conditions, trip length, and heading. Weather data will be automatically obtained using the OpenWeather API.

### User stories
- A user can enter the parameters of their bicycle and battery.
- Before taking a trip for the first time, a commuter can enter trip parameters to estimate a minimum charge for their battery.
- When weather conditions change, before their commute, a user can get a human-readable score (like an index) on how much battery level will be impacted.
- A user can find what speed they should maintain to arrive at their destination with a given battery level.
